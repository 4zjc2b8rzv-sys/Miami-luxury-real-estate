About Halden Villas

A Miami agency that sells only villas, for clients who want a beautiful home and an honest deal.

Our story

Elena and Marc Halden moved to Miami from Europe in 2008. Buying their own house was harder than it should have been: unclear prices, photos that hid problems and agents who disappeared after the deposit.

In 2010 they started Halden Villas to do it differently. Today we are a team of eight, and we have sold more than 140 villas to families from Europe, Latin America and the United States.

We still work the same way. We visit every villa ourselves, we say what we really think about it, and the same person looks after you until you have the keys, and afterwards if you need us.

How we work

We listen first

A short call to understand how you live, where you want to be and what you want to spend.

We choose for you

We send a few villas that truly fit, with real photos, a video tour and our honest opinion.

We handle the rest

Negotiation, inspection, lawyers, banks, insurance and, if you wish, rental and management.
